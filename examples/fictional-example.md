### Contingency Plan B (Holiday Pop-Up Sale — excerpt)

Gate 2 pre-decisions: price floor = no discount deeper than 25% off;
discount-code cap = 500 redemptions + kill switch held by the marketer;
dynamic QR in use; holding statement approved. Plan B decision-maker:
the marketer. Daily data collector: the marketer.

| Situation | Plan B (pre-decided) |
|---|---|
| Website / QR down | Switch to backup link (dynamic QR); extend promo by the downtime |
| Low turnout by mid-campaign | Social + email blast; extend promo |
| Promo product sells out | Substitute product; update website + social + store signage at once |
