# Campaign Plan Report — 2027 Annual Plan

Maple & Main Tea Co. · Time span: 2027-01-01 → 2027-12-31 · Country: Canada

## 1. Goal

Four campaigns across 2027 for local gift buyers and summer shoppers: Valentine's,
Mother's Day, Canada Day (with in-store tasting), and Holiday Season.

## 2. Campaign overview

Four campaigns, one per quarter. Anchors: Valentine's Day (Feb 14), Mother's Day
(May 9), Canada Day (Jul 1), Black Friday (Nov 26, promo runs Nov 26 – Dec 24).
Candidate dates considered at Gate 1 but NOT chosen: Father's Day (Jun 20),
Labour Day (Sep 6), Thanksgiving (Oct 11), Remembrance Day (Nov 11), Christmas
(Dec 25). Sep – Oct is an intentional gap — no campaign, by user choice.
