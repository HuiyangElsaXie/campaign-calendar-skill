# Research Protocol — Date Evidence

Research in Stage 1 fills DATE gaps, not the user's product, audience, business stage or season. Existing business facts are reused; conflicts with current explicit corrections are clarified rather than silently reconciled.

## Date ownership and confidence

- **user-set**: the user chooses their own anniversary / campaign date. Adopt it without external verification; validate the date syntax and scope only.
- **user-reported public date**: an external event / holiday date quoted by the user. Preserve that provenance; it is **unverified**, not an official fact, until checked.
- **base-listed**: a shipped checklist entry / rule. This does **not** mean that the target year's occurrence or local observance has been verified.
- **government-sourced**: the target-year date was read from a relevant government source.
- **official-announcement**: the current edition / window was read from the responsible organizer / retailer.
- **unverified**: no adequate current-year evidence, conflicting sources, or verification unavailable. TBC is valid.

A user may accept an unverified public date for a conditional draft; keep the label and unresolved dependency. Neither user selection nor Gate 2 upgrades it to an official or verified fact.

## When and what to research

Research a country without a shipped layer, relevant cultural dates, announced windows, local opportunities, and any date proposed from memory. Verify checklist occurrences for each year / region before a verified label. Public holidays and commercial anchors are different: include a date when it has a specific business / audience reason, not merely because it is statutory.

## Sources and procedure

1. Search the target year / edition and region. Open and read the page; a snippet alone is not evidence.
2. Use government calendars for statutory dates / local observance; organizer, federation or retailer pages for events and sale windows; relevant published calendars for commercial dates. A statutory-holiday list cannot verify every retail occasion.
3. For computed lunar / variable dates, record the rule and check two independent reliable sources that agree. Do not copy a lunar occurrence across countries.
4. Use dated city / BIA / community-organizer listings for local events. Avoid content farms, AI listicles, undated pages and unverified reposts. Last year's edition is a historical lead, not proof of recurrence or a current edition.
5. Conflicting evidence => show the conflict, keep unverified, and ask the user which planning assumption to accept. No reliable result => TBC / unverified.

For each researched date record **event/year, exact date or window, region/timezone, source URL (or supplied source identifier), page publication/update date if stated, date checked, and confidence**. Do not invent a publication date. Put the record in a source column / footnote, referenced by the candidate ID. Evidence of an event date does not prove booth availability, registration eligibility, price or an application deadline.

Research-built country layers are shown to the user for confirmation before use and labelled as such. No external source is needed for the user's own business facts; do not reuse another client's private data.

## Offline / unavailable tools

Use available search tools without assuming a browser or a paid service. If research is unavailable, say so and use supplied sources or base-listed rules only as **unverified / TBC** occurrences. Ask for missing dates if they affect selection; never promote a historical spot-check or remembered date to current verification. No web access is a supported conditional-draft path, not permission to fabricate evidence.
